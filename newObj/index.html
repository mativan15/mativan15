Drag empty space to look around. Right-drag moves the view, the scroll wheel zooms. Drag the glass lens to move it, hold Alt to push it nearer or farther, arrow keys nudge it, Z and X spin it, C and V tilt it. The small dot is the lamp, not the lens: drag it to aim the rays, hold Shift to slide the bundle, I and K tilt the rays, J and L turn them, U and O slide them. W A S D and Q E move the camera. The [ key makes the room darker, the ] key makes it brighter. Comma and period change the glass blur. Minus and equals change how much the glass darkens the view. R shows or hides the brass ring. P puts everything back.
